The template says each member talks about their own role. The roles below are a suggestion. Change them to match who really did what.

Total about 19 minutes. Rehearse once and keep a safety margin under 20.

## Demo checklist
1. Run travelx_full_database.sql in MySQL Workbench. No errors.
2. Show the EER and physical diagram.
3. Trigger demo: book room 1 for overlapping dates in the front end (or run test T1). Show the error "Room already booked for these dates". Then book back-to-back dates and show it works.
4. Run each view, then 4 to 6 queries with their comment on screen.
5. Each member says their name and what they did.
